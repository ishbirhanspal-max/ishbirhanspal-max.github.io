How to Add Text to a PDF

PDFs are fixed-layout page descriptions where text is stored as precisely positioned character glyphs with embedded font data. There is no concept of "paragraphs" that reflow — even Adobe Acrobat Pro struggles with text editing and frequently breaks surrounding formatting. The practical solution is text overlays: your new text renders on top of existing PDF content as a transparent annotation layer, positioned exactly where you click. The result looks visually identical to edited text.

  1. Open the OptiPixel PDF Editor
  2. Upload your PDF by dragging to the drop zone or clicking Browse
  3. Select the Add Text tool from the toolbar
  4. Click anywhere on the page to place a text box at that position
  5. Type your text. Adjust font size, colour, and family using the format controls
  6. Drag the text box to fine-tune its position
  7. Add multiple text boxes across multiple pages as needed
  8. Click Download PDF to save your edited version

How to Sign a PDF Electronically for Free

Electronic signatures are accepted for the vast majority of business purposes — contracts, invoices, letters, approval documents, and internal forms. OptiPixel provides three signature options:

  • Draw Your Signature: Use mouse, trackpad, or touchscreen to draw on the signature pad. Captured as a transparent PNG and placed anywhere on your PDF. Creates a natural handwritten look.
  • Upload a Signature Image: Have a scanned or photographed image of your handwritten signature? Upload it as PNG, then use the Background Remover to remove the white background first for a clean transparent signature.
  • Typed Signature: Type your name in a cursive or calligraphic font. Accepted for most business correspondence, especially when combined with a timestamp.

How to Annotate and Highlight PDFs

The OptiPixel PDF editor supports multiple annotation tools for document review, contract mark-up, and team collaboration:

  • Highlight tool: Draw translucent yellow (or custom colour) rectangles over text to mark important passages. The text beneath remains selectable and searchable.
  • Freehand drawing: Pen tool for freehand annotations, arrows, circles, or underlines in any colour and stroke width. Ideal for marking up diagrams and technical drawings.
  • Shape stamps: Add bordered rectangles, circles, or custom shapes to highlight regions or section off areas.
  • Image stamps: Add image overlays — company logos, APPROVED stamps, CONFIDENTIAL overlays, or any custom graphic asset.

How to Merge Multiple PDFs

Consolidating multiple invoices into a single submission, combining scanned pages, or assembling a report from multiple sections all require PDF merging. The process is straightforward:

  1. Open the PDF Editor and select Merge PDFs from the mode dropdown
  2. Upload all PDFs you want to combine (select multiple files at once)
  3. Drag the file cards to reorder documents into your desired sequence
  4. Click Process and Download PDF — the merged file downloads instantly

All merging uses the pdf-lib library entirely in your browser. No file size limits (beyond your device RAM), no page count limits, and no server involvement.

How to Split a PDF by Page Range

  1. Select Split PDF mode in the PDF Editor
  2. Upload your PDF and enter the page range to extract, e.g., "1-3" for pages 1 through 3, or "2,5,7" for specific individual pages
  3. Click Process and Download PDF

Useful for: extracting specific chapters from a large report, separating individual forms from a combined submission, breaking a large scanned document into individual sections for separate filing.

How to Add a Text Watermark to a PDF

Text watermarks (CONFIDENTIAL, DRAFT, DO NOT DISTRIBUTE, CLIENT COPY) are applied as repeating diagonal text across all pages. Select Watermark mode, enter your text, choose colour (typically light grey or red) and opacity (20–30% recommended), select a 45° diagonal angle, and download. The watermark is rendered as a PDF annotation layer for clean visual integration.

How to Password-Protect a PDF

OptiPixel uses AES-128 encryption — the same standard used by enterprise document management systems and strong enough for all typical business documents. Select Password Protect mode, upload your PDF, enter and confirm a strong password (8+ characters, mixed case, numbers, symbols recommended), and download. All encryption happens locally in your browser — the password and document bytes are never transmitted to any server.

💡 Security tip: Store the password separately from the encrypted document. Never include the password in the email alongside the attached PDF. Use a password manager to store document passwords securely.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is an electronic signature drawn in a browser legally binding?

In most jurisdictions, yes — an electronic signature is legally equivalent to a wet ink signature for most business purposes under the Indian Information Technology Act 2000, the US ESIGN Act, and EU eIDAS regulation. Specific high-stakes transactions (property transfers, court documents, wills) may require certified digital signatures with cryptographic verification. Check requirements for your specific use case before relying on a simple electronic signature for critical legal documents.

Can I edit a PDF created from a scanned document?

A scanned PDF contains images of each page. You can add text overlays on top of the scan, but you cannot directly edit the scanned text content. Use the OCR Text Scanner to extract text from scanned documents, edit it in a word processor, and create a new PDF from the edited text.

Why can't I edit the original text directly in a PDF?

PDFs store text as precisely positioned glyphs with embedded font files, not as editable text flows. Editing existing text requires finding font matches, re-typesetting surrounding content, and reflowing paragraphs — operations that even Adobe Acrobat Pro struggles with for non-trivial edits. For most practical needs, adding a matching text overlay achieves the same visual result more reliably and quickly.

What happens to my PDF when I close the browser tab?

All processing happens in your browser's temporary memory (RAM). Closing the tab immediately deallocates all memory associated with your PDF. There is no persistent storage, no server-side copy, and no way for anyone to recover the file. OptiPixel Studio operates with zero server storage for user documents.
