Tips for the Best Passport Photo

  • Face the camera directly with a neutral expression (slight smile is acceptable for many countries)
  • Ensure your face is clearly visible — eyes open, hair away from face
  • Use good, even lighting to avoid shadows on your face or background
  • Remove glasses (most countries require glasses-free photos since 2023)
  • Print on glossy photo paper at a photo lab for best results

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

No. Your photo is processed entirely inside your browser using the HTML5 Canvas API. No image data is sent to any server. Your privacy is completely protected.

Can I use this for Indian passport applications?

Yes. Select "EU / India Passport (35×45mm)" from the dropdown. The standard for Indian passport photos is 35mm × 45mm with a white background, which our tool produces perfectly.

Can I print directly from the browser?

Yes! Download the PNG sheet and print it at your local photo lab or home printer on 4×6 inch glossy photo paper. Set print settings to "actual size" with no scaling to ensure correct dimensions.
