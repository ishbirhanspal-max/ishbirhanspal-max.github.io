Why Use OptiPixel's Video to GIF Converter?

Animated GIFs are the universal language of social media, messaging apps, and online documentation. Frequently Asked Questions

What video formats are supported?

You can convert standard MP4 (H.264), WebM (VP8/VP9), and Apple QuickTime MOV files directly on Windows, macOS, Android, and iOS.

How do I keep my GIF file size small?

GIF files can become quite large if they contain too many frames. For smaller file sizes that load quickly on web pages, select 320px width, a duration under 4 seconds, and 10 FPS.

Can I use the generated GIF commercially?

Yes, absolutely. OptiPixel Studio is completely free for both personal and commercial use without attribution requirements.
