Preset Filter Descriptions

⚡ Vivid Boost

Cranks up contrast and saturation by 30–40% for vivid, punchy photos that pop on social media. Ideal for nature, food, and product shots.

🌅 Sunset Warm

Warms the hue by 15° and boosts saturation with a soft brightness lift. Creates a golden-hour feel without needing to re-shoot.

❄️ Cool Breeze

Shifts hue 210° towards blue-violet tones and reduces saturation slightly. Perfect for moody urban or winter photography.

📽️ Vintage Film

Applies sepia toning, reduces contrast, and softens brightness to simulate an authentic film photography aesthetic from the 1970s.

🌃 Cyberpunk

Pushes saturation to maximum with a purple hue shift and high contrast. Creates a neon-lit futuristic look inspired by sci-fi cinema.

🎞️ Film Noir (B&W)

Converts to black and white by eliminating saturation, with boosted contrast for dramatic shadows. The classic cinematic look.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are filters applied destructively?

No. The original image is kept in memory throughout your session. You can switch presets, reset sliders, and re-apply any combination of filters without quality loss until you download.

Can I combine presets with manual slider adjustments?

Yes! Clicking a preset sets the sliders to its default values. You can then fine-tune any individual slider on top of the preset for a fully custom look.

What format is the download?

The filtered image is downloaded as a high-quality PNG, preserving all pixel detail at the original image resolution.
