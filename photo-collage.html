Frequently Asked Questions

How does Click-to-Swap work?

Simply click the "⇄ Swap" button on any populated photo slot, then click any other slot. Their images and framing adjustments will immediately swap positions.

Can I copy the collage without saving a file?

Yes! Click "📋 Copy to Clipboard" to copy the rendered image directly. You can paste it into WhatsApp Web, Slack, Discord, Twitter/X, or Canva using Ctrl + V.
