3. Accuracy of Outputs and AI Limitations

Several tools on OptiPixel Studio employ machine learning models or heuristic algorithms to produce their outputs. While these models are built on established, peer-reviewed computer vision and image processing research, they are not infallible.

Background Removal Accuracy

Our background removal tool uses the MediaPipe Selfie Segmentation model, which is optimized for portrait photography with clearly contrasting backgrounds. The model may produce suboptimal results for images with complex, multi-coloured, or patterned backgrounds that closely match foreground subject colours, photos with unusual lighting, heavy shadows, or extreme backlighting, non-portrait subjects such as animals, vehicles, or abstract objects, and very low-resolution images below 200 by 200 pixels. In such cases, we recommend re-trying with better-lit photos, or using the output as a base for further manual editing in a desktop image editor such as GIMP or Adobe Photoshop.

Compression Ratio Estimates

File size estimates shown during image or PDF compression reflect typical results for JPEG-encoded photographs under standard compression settings. The actual output size depends on the entropy and information density of each individual image and cannot be guaranteed to match any specific target without iterative adjustment.

QR Code and Barcode Generation

QR codes and barcodes generated by OptiPixel Studio follow the ISO/IEC 18004 and GS1 specifications respectively. However, physical printing conditions, ink spread, scanner hardware quality, and label surface finish can affect real-world scan success rates. Always test-scan generated codes before mass production or deployment on physical products or packaging.

6. OCR Text Recognition Limitations

The OCR Text Scanner extracts machine-readable text from scanned documents, screenshots, and photographs. This functionality is powered by Tesseract.js v4, a WebAssembly-compiled version of the Tesseract open-source OCR engine originally developed at HP and maintained by Google.

Users should be aware that accuracy is highest for English Latin-script text. Multi-language documents mixing English with non-Latin scripts such as Arabic, Chinese, or Devanagari may require selecting the correct language pack to achieve acceptable accuracy. Tesseract.js is trained on printed fonts and performs poorly on cursive or handwritten text. The minimum recommended scan resolution is 300 DPI, as images below 200 DPI will produce significantly degraded character recognition accuracy. Multi-column documents, tables, and forms with irregular text flow may not extract in the correct reading order.

OptiPixel Studio expressly disclaims any liability arising from errors in OCR-extracted text that are subsequently used in official communications, legal documents, financial submissions, or other high-stakes applications without human verification.

7. PDF Compression and Conversion Quality

Our PDF Compressor and Image to PDF tools use client-side JavaScript PDF generation libraries. PDF compression results depend on the content type of the source document. PDFs containing high-resolution photographic imagery will compress significantly, while PDFs consisting primarily of vector text or line graphics will compress very little since the content is already optimally encoded.

When a PDF is re-encoded through our compressor, embedded font subsets may be altered. For critical typography-sensitive documents such as legal contracts with specific font requirements, always verify font rendering in the output PDF before distribution. Password-protected or DRM-encrypted PDFs cannot be opened or processed by our tools. PDF documents containing JavaScript actions, interactive form fields, digital signatures, or multimedia embeds may lose these interactive elements when processed through our compression pipeline.
