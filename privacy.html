Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026 | Effective date: September 10, 2026

🛡️ Key Promise: Your files (images, PDFs, documents) are processed 100% inside your browser. Nothing is ever uploaded to our servers. We have no servers that receive your files.

📋 1. Who We Are

OptiPixel Studio ("we", "us", "our") operates the website optipixelstudio.online (also hosted at ishbirhanspal-max.github.io). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our free online tools.

For privacy-related questions, you may contact us via our GitHub repository.

💾 2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any of the following:

  • Images, photos, or any uploaded files
  • PDF documents or Word files
  • Extracted text from OCR processing
  • QR code or barcode content
  • Audio recordings
  • Your name, email address, or account information (we have no accounts)
  • Your location beyond what your browser already exposes

All tool processing (image compression, background removal, PDF editing, OCR, QR/barcode generation, etc.) happens exclusively inside your browser using JavaScript, HTML5 Canvas, and WebAssembly. No files ever leave your device.

📊 3. Information We DO Collect (Automatically)

Like most websites, our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) and analytics tools may automatically collect:

  • Log data: Your IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URL, pages visited, and time of visit — collected by GitHub Pages infrastructure
  • Usage analytics: Page views and general site usage patterns (via anonymized analytics)

This data is used solely to understand general traffic patterns and improve the site. It is not linked to your identity and is not used for remarketing.

🍪 4. Cookies & Local Storage

We use the following cookies and browser storage:

  • Cookie Consent Storage (localStorage): We store optipixel_cookie_consent in your browser's localStorage to remember your cookie preference. This is a first-party, essential function and cannot be disabled.
  • Google AdSense Cookies: If you accept advertising cookies, Google may set cookies to serve relevant ads. Google's ad cookies track your general browsing interests (not your files) across websites to serve personalized ads.
  • Google Analytics Cookies: If enabled, Google Analytics sets cookies to measure aggregate website traffic.

You can control advertising cookies via our cookie consent banner shown when you first visit the site. You may also opt out of Google's interest-based advertising at google.com/settings/ads.

📢 5. Google AdSense & Advertising

We use Google AdSense (Publisher ID: ca-pub-9875981872644926) to display advertisements on this site. Google AdSense uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to serve ads based on your browsing history and interests.

Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on our site. Google's use of the DoubleClick cookie enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our site and other sites. You may opt out of the use of the DoubleClick cookie by visiting the Google Ads Settings page.

Ads are clearly labeled as "Advertisement" or "Sponsored" on our website. We do not place ads in a way that is misleading or designed to be confused with our tool content.

For more information on Google AdSense and privacy, see: Google Privacy Policy and AdSense Privacy Policy.

🔗 6. Third-Party Services

Our tools use the following third-party JavaScript libraries loaded from CDNs. These CDNs may set their own cookies or log requests:

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com) — Serves the Outfit and JetBrains Mono fonts
  • cdnjs.cloudflare.com — Serves JSZip, PDF.js, QRCode.js libraries
  • unpkg.com — Serves pdf-lib.js library
  • cdn.jsdelivr.net — Serves MediaPipe (background removal) and Tesseract.js (OCR)

These services have their own privacy policies. We recommend reviewing them if you are concerned about CDN data collection.

👶 7. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Our service is intended for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. We do not have user accounts, registration, or any mechanism that would allow us to collect personal data from children.

Google AdSense is configured to serve general audience ads. We have not enabled interest-based ads targeting children.

🌍 8. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

If you are a resident of the European Union (GDPR) or California (CCPA), you have the right to:

  • Know what personal data we collect (see Section 3)
  • Opt out of advertising cookies using our cookie consent banner
  • Opt out of Google's personalized advertising at adssettings.google.com
  • Clear your local cookie consent preference by clearing your browser's localStorage for this site

Since we do not collect personal data directly, we cannot fulfill data deletion or access requests for user-specific data — because we simply don't have any.

🔄 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.
