Frequently Asked Questions

Why didn't my file upload?

OptiPixel Studio does not upload your files anywhere! All files are loaded directly into your browser's local RAM. If a large file fails to process, check that your device has sufficient free RAM (especially on mobile devices with PDFs over 50MB).

Is OptiPixel Studio truly free to use for commercial projects?

Yes, 100%. All images, PDFs, barcodes, QR codes, and OCR transcripts generated by OptiPixel Studio are yours to use for personal, commercial, and enterprise applications with zero attribution required.

How can I suggest a new tool for the studio?

Submit a feature request via the form above or open an issue on our GitHub repository. We love building new client-side utilities!
