Indian Passport Photo Requirements (2024–2025)

The Ministry of External Affairs (MEA) specifies the following requirements for Indian passport photos:

  • Size: 51mm × 51mm (2 × 2 inches). The face should cover 70–80% of the photo height.
  • Background: Plain white or off-white background — no patterns, shadows, or gradients.
  • Expression: Neutral expression with mouth closed. Eyes fully open and clearly visible.
  • Pose: Front-facing, no head tilt. Both ears must be visible. Hair should not cover the face.
  • Glasses: Glasses are not permitted in Indian passport photos as of 2023 MEA guidelines.
  • Headwear: Only permitted for religious reasons. Must not cast shadows on the face.
  • Print Quality: 300 DPI (dots per inch) minimum for printed photos. JPEG format for digital submissions.
  • File Size (digital): Minimum 10KB, maximum 1MB for Passport Seva Portal uploads.
  • Age: The photo must have been taken within the last 6 months and reflect current appearance.

Passport Seva Portal Upload Requirements

When applying for an Indian passport online through passportindia.gov.in, you need to upload a digital photo. Here are the exact technical requirements:

  • Format: JPEG only
  • Minimum size: 10 KB
  • Maximum size: 1 MB (1000 KB)
  • Minimum pixel dimensions: 200 × 200 pixels
  • Maximum pixel dimensions: 1000 × 1000 pixels
  • Colour mode: RGB (not grayscale or CMYK)

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the size of a passport photo in India?

The Indian passport requires a 51mm × 51mm (2 × 2 inch) photo. For visa applications to other countries (including Schengen countries, UAE, and Australia), the required size is typically 35mm × 45mm. Always check the latest guidelines on the specific embassy or consulate website for visa applications, as requirements can vary.

Can I use a photo taken on my phone for a passport?

Yes. Modern smartphones (iPhone 12+, Samsung Galaxy S21+, Pixel 6+) produce photos at 12–50 megapixels, which is more than sufficient for passport photo requirements. The key is good lighting (natural daylight, no harsh shadows) and a plain white or light background. Our tool handles all the resizing and formatting automatically.

Is it legal to make my own passport photo?

Yes, it is completely legal in India and most countries to prepare your own passport photo at home, provided it meets the official specifications. There is no requirement to use a professional photographer. The authorities evaluate the photo on whether it meets the technical and visual requirements — not on who took it.

How many photos do I need for an Indian passport application?

For a fresh Indian passport application, you need 2 identical colour passport photos — one to paste on the application form and one to attach. For a police verification visit, you may need 1–2 additional photos. Keep a digital copy of the final photo for the Passport Seva portal upload.
