Why Do Portals Demand PDFs Under 100KB or 200KB?

Whether you are filling out a college registration form, submitting an employment background verification, or uploading documents for visa processing, portals frequently mandate:

🎓 University Admissions

Strict 100KB–200KB PDF limits for 10th/12th grade mark sheets and certificates.

🏛️ Government Job Forms

UPSC, SSC, State PSC portals require caste/category certificates under 100KB PDF.

🏦 Banking & KYC

Bank loan and KYC portals reject Aadhaar / PAN card scans that exceed 200KB.

💼 Job Applications

Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) and email attachments prefer compact PDFs under 200KB.

Tips for Crystal-Clear Text at Small PDF File Sizes

  • Shoot in Bright Sunlight or Under a Desk Lamp: Good lighting eliminates sensor noise in camera photos, allowing image compression algorithms to achieve 50% smaller sizes without blur.
  • Crop Out Desks and Backgrounds: Crop tightly around the edges of the paper before converting. Unnecessary wooden desk textures consume file size that should be reserved for your text.
  • Use Black & White or Grayscale for Text Documents: Color data accounts for 66% of photo file size. Grayscale documents compress down to under 50KB with razor-sharp readability.
