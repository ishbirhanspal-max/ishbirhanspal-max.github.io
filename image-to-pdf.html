How to Convert Images to PDF for Free

  1. Select Your Images: Click the drop zone or drag and drop JPG, PNG, WebP, or GIF files. Add as many images as you need.
  2. Arrange Page Order: Drag image thumbnails to reorder pages before conversion.
  3. Choose Page Settings: Pick your page size (A4, Letter, A3), orientation (portrait/landscape), and margin size.
  4. Click "Convert to PDF": Your browser converts all images into a professional multi-page PDF using the PDF-lib JavaScript library.
  5. Download: Save your PDF file instantly — no email required, no watermarks, no file-size limit.

Why Use a Client-Side Image to PDF Converter?

Most online PDF converters upload your images to a remote server in a different country. This is a significant privacy issue when converting personal photos, ID scans, legal documents, or business invoices. OptiPixel Studio uses PDF-lib, a pure JavaScript PDF generation library, which runs entirely inside your browser. Your images are never transmitted over the internet — they stay on your device the entire time.

Supported Image Formats
