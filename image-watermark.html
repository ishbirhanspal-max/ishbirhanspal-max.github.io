Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use transparent PNG logos as watermarks?

Yes! OptiPixel Studio's batch watermarker fully respects alpha-transparency in PNG and SVG logos. Upload your logo with a transparent background, adjust the scale and opacity, and position it anywhere on your photos.

Will adding a watermark lower the quality of my original images?

No. We render the watermark directly onto the original pixel dimensions of your uploaded image. An 18 megapixel camera photo remains crisp, full resolution, and ready for high-grade commercial display.

What is the difference between single placement and tiled mode?

Single placement stamps a discreet copyright mark or logo into one specific corner (such as the bottom-right or center). Tiled mode repeats the mark across the entire canvas at an angle, making it virtually impossible for image scrapers to crop out your copyright tag.
