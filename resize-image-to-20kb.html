Why Online Application Portals Reject Uploaded Images

Over 35% of exam form submission errors occur at the document upload step. Here are the 4 most common reasons and how to avoid them:

  • Exceeding Maximum File Size (e.g., "File exceeds 50 KB"): High-resolution phone cameras produce images between 2MB and 8MB. Uploading without prior compression triggers an automatic error. Use our Image Compressor to reduce file size.
  • Below Minimum File Size (e.g., "File is smaller than 10 KB"): Over-compressed images with blocky compression artifacts are rejected by automatic OCR scanners. Always keep quality at 70% or higher.
  • Wrong Aspect Ratio or Distorted Face: Squishing or stretching a portrait to match arbitrary pixels without proportional cropping will disqualify your admit card. Use our Passport Photo Maker for automatic face centering.
  • Dark or Patterned Background: Government rules strictly mandate a plain white or light off-white background. If you took your photo against a wall or curtain, use our Free Background Remover to replace it with pure white.
